# Labor Law Quick Reference (Vietnam)

Law of record: **Labor Code: Law No. 44/2025/QH15, effective 01/01/2025**
(replaces the 2019 law). Cite articles from the 2025 law.

## Mandatory workflow
1. Match the question to the quick table below.
2. **Before advising**: open the current consolidated text (Văn bản hợp
   nhất) of the 2025 law and verify the article number + exact wording.
3. Only then answer; cite "Điều X, Luật 44/2025/QH15".
4. For specific case decisions (compensation amounts, disputes) — recommend
   consulting a lawyer; output an analysis, not advice.

## Quick table (verify each item before advising — column "Citation" holds
the 2019-law article; find its 2025-law counterpart by subject matter)
| Topic | Rule (verify before citing) | 2019 ref |
|---|---|---|
| Probation cap | ≤ 6 months for positions requiring 4-year university degree or higher; 3 months for college/vocational; 1 month for other jobs (Art. 26/2019) | verify 2025 |
| Probation salary | ≥ 85% of the position's regular wage (Art. 26/2019) | verify 2025 |

| Social insurance | Mandatory from day 1 for contracts of 1 month or more (BHXH Law 2014/2024) | — |

## Red flags in labor contracts
- Probation exceeding the cap, or repeating probation for the same position.
- Probation salary under 85% (or no explicit probation wage).
- Overtime without the employee's written consent.
- Unilateral penalty clause against the employee.
- Missing dispute resolution clause (labor arbitration vs. court).
